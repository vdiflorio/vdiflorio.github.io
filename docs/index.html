About

I’m a mathematical physicist who works on both theory and its practical applications, from non-equilibrium statistical mechanics to numerical solvers for real proteins.

I hold a Ph.D. in Pure and Applied Mathematics from Politecnico di Torino and the Istituto Italiano di Tecnologia. Today I work on protein–protein interactions and binding affinity through continuum electrostatics, and on the numerical solvers that make those calculations fast and accurate, starting from NextGenPB, the Poisson–Boltzmann solver I developed with my co-authors.

I also work on non-equilibrium statistical mechanics. If you are interested in collaborations in computational physics, biomolecular modelling or high-performance scientific software, write to me.

Research

  • Protein–protein binding

    How proteins recognise and bind each other, studied through continuum electrostatics: electrostatic complementarity at biomolecular interfaces, interaction forces and binding affinity.

  • Poisson–Boltzmann solvers

    Efficient numerical methods for the Poisson–Boltzmann equation. NextGenPB uses finite elements, analytical super-resolution and a mesh refined only where the molecular surface is. I also work on data-driven surrogates that speed these calculations up.

  • Non-equilibrium statistical mechanics

    The behaviour of low-dimensional systems far from equilibrium, from heat conduction in one-dimensional chains to the Boltzmann equation.

Projects

  • NextGenPB

    Solver for biomolecular electrostatics based on the Poisson–Boltzmann equation, with analytical super-resolution and local mesh refinement. Integrated in the MCCE workflow for pKa calculations. CodeTutorialPaper

  • Analytical electrostatics

    Analytical tools for the electrostatic modelling of macromolecules and proteins in electrolytic environments. Code

  • 1D chains

    Numerical simulation of one-dimensional chain models for non-equilibrium dynamics and statistical mechanics. CodePaper
