Research interests

Protein–protein binding through continuum electrostatics, with a focus on electrostatic complementarity at biomolecular interfaces, and efficient numerical solvers for the Poisson–Boltzmann equation. I also develop data-driven and physics-guided neural surrogates that accelerate electrostatic calculations.

Non-equilibrium statistical dynamics and the behaviour of low-dimensional systems.
